SELECT-like 5-year MACE Risk Calculator

Estimation of individual 5-year risk of major adverse cardiovascular events in SELECT-like patients.

Designed for patients discharged after an ACS who have BMI ≥27 kg/m² and do not have diabetes. This tool is intended for risk stratification and does not estimate the individual treatment effect.

Prespecified threshold: 33%.
Risk <33%: Low-risk SELECT-like. Risk ≥33%: High-risk SELECT-like.
Model: flexible parametric survival model with a 5-year prediction horizon. Formula: risk = 100 × [1 − 0.9557053exp(LP)].
